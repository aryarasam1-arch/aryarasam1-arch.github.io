01 / SELECTED STUDIES

Rooms with
a point of view.

Illustrative design studies, not completed client projects. Each one explores a different way of using light and structure.

  1. 01

    House for a courtyard

    A compact home organised around one open sky. Circulation becomes a slow walk around the light.

  2. 02

    Reading room, north light

    A quiet public room where the windows do the work. Shelves are kept low so the light travels far.

  3. 03

    Two volumes, one studio

    A workspace split between a loud making room and a quiet thinking room, joined by a narrow threshold.

02 / APPROACH

Three questions
before a drawing.

01

How is it used?

We start with the ordinary day: where people pause, gather, work and rest. The plan follows from that.

02

Where is the light?

Orientation, shade and reflection decide more about a room than decoration ever will.

03

What lasts?

Materials are chosen for how they age. A surface should look better after ten years, not worse.

03 / STUDIO

“A room should give something back — quiet, shade, a place to put your coat.”

FOLD is a small, independent practice. We take on a limited number of homes and small public rooms each year, and we stay involved from the first sketch to the final detail.
