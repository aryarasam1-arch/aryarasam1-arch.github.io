What we treat

Preventive care first. If something needs treatment, you get the options, the costs and the trade-offs in writing before you decide.

01

Check-ups & cleaning

Routine examination, scale and polish, and a written note of anything to watch.

02

Fillings & repairs

Tooth-coloured fillings, cracked-tooth repair and same-week emergency slots.

03

Whitening

In-chair whitening with a shade assessment first, plus take-home top-up kits.

04

Braces & aligners

Metal, ceramic and clear aligner options with a full timeline and cost breakdown.

05

Children's dentistry

Short, calm appointments for under-12s, with sealants and fluoride where useful.

06

Root canal therapy

Single-visit where the case allows, with clear aftercare instructions.
