03 / ABOUT

A tidy desk,
in writing.

Deshmukh & Co. Tax & Advisory is a fictional practice created as an independent design study. It exists to show how a website can make paperwork feel less intimidating through clarity rather than claims.

The site describes a way of working — short explanations, tidy records and a clear list of what is needed next. Every detail, including the service list, is illustrative.

Nothing on this page is professional tax advice. No registrations or credentials are represented.
