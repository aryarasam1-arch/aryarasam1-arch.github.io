02 / STORY

A small room
with a long morning.

Kora Roasters & Kitchen is a fictional café created as an independent design study. It exists to show how a café website can feel warm and unhurried without stock photography or exaggerated claims.

We imagine a space where the barista knows the regular order, the kitchen writes the day's breakfast on a small board, and nobody is in a hurry to leave.

  • Coffee brewed to order
  • A short menu, changed often
  • Room to read and stay awhile
